[1] IS rgb(…,…,16) REACHABLE BY ANY CONVEX BLEND OF 17 AND 35?

    a convex blend is  out = D + a(S - D)  with a in [0, 1], D = 17, S = 35.
    S > D, so the blend is MONOTONE INCREASING in a and its range is [17, 35].
    over all 256 alpha codes, under round, trunc AND floor: any value below 17?  NO
    → GRÜN's tell holds. The mixing is not performed on the straight 8-bit values.

    variant                      B at k=1   (GRÜN measured 16)      R      G
    premul round, out trunc        16   MATCHES               6    12
    premul round, out round        17                         7    13
    premul trunc, out trunc        16   MATCHES               5    11
    premul trunc, out round        17                         6    12
    straight, out round            17                         7    13
    straight, out trunc            17                         6    12

    Mechanism, stated so it can be refuted: at k = 1 the premultiplied source is
      B: round(35 x 1/255)  = round(0.1373) = 0      — the source's contribution is DISCARDED
      and the backdrop term is 17 x 254/255 = 16.9333  — the backdrop is still ATTENUATED
    So a bright source at low alpha over a dark backdrop makes it DARKER. That is the whole tell:
    premultiplication quantises the source's contribution to zero while the (1 - a) term survives.
