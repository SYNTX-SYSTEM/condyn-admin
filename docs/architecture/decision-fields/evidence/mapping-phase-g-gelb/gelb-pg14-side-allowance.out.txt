GELB · PG-14's THIRD LABEL — the collar's "more" text is left-anchored and unbought
====================================================================================================

The fit condition. Writing rpx for the body radius in CSS px (so r = rpx * k):
  allowance  side   >= collar = r + 2.6k + max(2.4k, 0.18r) + 3.6k
  required   reach   = collarR + 3k + width = r + 2.6k + 3k + chars * EM * font
  so it fits iff     chars * EM * 11 * 1.002 * k  <=  max(2.4k, 0.18r) + 0.6k

  Taking the 0.18r branch (it dominates for every rpx >= 14):
    chars <= (0.18 * rpx + 0.6) / (EM * 11 * 1.002)

  rpx   diameter   chars that FIT (MONO 0.602)   chars that FIT (product's 0.62)
   28     56 px       0.850                        0.825
   32     64 px       0.959                        0.931
   36     72 px       1.067                        1.036
   40     80 px       1.176                        1.141
   46     92 px       1.338                        1.299

  PG-3's stated range is a 56…92 px diameter at 1920, i.e. rpx 28…46.
  → the budget is ONE CHARACTER at the smallest body and under one and a half at the largest.
  → any real copy for "n more" overruns the frame on the right, at L1, whenever a collar hides
    an arc. The label is left-anchored, so it overruns by nearly its whole width.

  Why neither route reached it:
    · GRÜN's sweep models 0.62 * title.length on both sides of its comparison, so the constant
      cancels and the title is the only string in it (PINK's A-G244 point, one label further).
    · PINK's stand is L0, and this text renders only at L1 with collar.hidden > 0.
    · It is a DERIVATION here, not a measurement — it needs the copy string and A-G51's L1
      fixture to become a reading. Recorded as a derivation with its assumptions named.
