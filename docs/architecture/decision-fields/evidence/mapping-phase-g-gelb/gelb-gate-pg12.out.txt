  CONSEQUENCE for A-G128, and it is the finding:
    A-G124's failing observation is "medium pixels at a given radius adjacent to a GAP body's glow
    carrying measurably more chroma than medium pixels at the same radius adjacent to a NON-GAP
    body's glow". Taken strictly OUTSIDE the gap glow's own circle the projection is identically
    zero by construction, so there is no failing observation; taken INSIDE it, the pin fires on the
    glow itself, which is the mirror defect A-G126 rejected the magnitude form for.

    The region that admits BOTH is the one neither reading names: where a gap body's glow circle
    OVERLAPS the angular neighbourhood of a non-gap body. The glow runs to 2.6–3.0 × the drawn
    radius while A-G1's collision search only guarantees the DRAWN radii do not touch — so glow
    overlap is permitted by the layout and is the only mechanism that can place gap chroma where
    no gap body is. PG-12 is therefore satisfiable, and what it actually tests is glow overlap.

  THE DATUM THE GATE STILL NEEDS — stated narrowly, per A-G132, by the requester:
    one scalar: the MAXIMUM gap-glow alpha landing on a medium pixel whose nearest drawn body is a
    NON-GAP body, over the exercised layouts, at L0/1920 and L1. It is a layout computation from
    `layout-spec-v1.1.json` and the fixtures — no render, no raster, no credential. Not the chroma,
    not a verdict, not a per-layout table: one alpha.

  Bounds on PINK's factor once that scalar exists, in q_proj:
