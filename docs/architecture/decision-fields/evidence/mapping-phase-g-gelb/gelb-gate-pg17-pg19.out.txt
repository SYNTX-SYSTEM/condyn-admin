[6] PG-19 — what R25 actually quantifies
    R25, verbatim: "enhanced when the stimulus is small, low-contrast or blurred, and enhanced
    further from fixation. Raising target contrast halves the disappearance rate."
    the halving fixes ONE exponent: rate ~ C^k with 2^k = 1/2  =>  k = -1.000000
    size: direction only, no exponent.   eccentricity: direction only, no exponent.
    => a joint scalar index needs an exchange rate between factors. R25 supplies none.
